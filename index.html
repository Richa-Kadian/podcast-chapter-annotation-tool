What Momento actually does

Fewer tools, wired together properly.

Not a menu of twenty disconnected AI utilities — one pipeline where each piece feeds the next.

Synced transcript

Every line highlights itself in real time as audio plays, found with a hand-coded binary search.

Auto-detected chapters

Topic shifts are found by tracking keyword frequency, not typed in by hand.

Search into playback

Type a word, jump straight to the second it was actually said.

Timestamp-linked quiz

Every question remembers the exact line it came from — wrong answers jump back to it.

Streaks & badges

Attempts, scores, and day-over-day streaks persist per recording in this browser.
